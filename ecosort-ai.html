Eco tips

Waste segregation tips

Empty and rinse

Food-soiled packaging can spoil a whole batch of clean recycling. A quick rinse helps.

Keep food waste separate

Compost or use a food-waste bin so scraps don’t contaminate dry recyclables.

Check local rules

Accepted materials differ by city and council. Always check your local guide.

When in doubt, leave it out

One wrong item can contaminate a bin. Ask your local service if unsure.

PROTOTYPE

This is a prototype

EcoSort AI is a design concept made for a university Environmental Studies assignment. No genuine AI image recognition is implemented. The sample results are pre-written demonstrations, and uploaded photos are only previewed on your own device.

The problem

People often put items in the wrong bin because the rules are confusing. Contaminated recycling can be rejected, and recoverable materials and food waste end up in landfill.

How AI could help

A trained image-recognition model could look at a photo, suggest the likely material, and show local disposal steps in seconds, right when someone is holding the item.

Possible benefits

Cleaner recycling streams, less landfill, lower methane from food waste, and better everyday habits. These are potential benefits that would need real testing.
